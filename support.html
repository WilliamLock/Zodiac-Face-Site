Quick help

The camera cannot find my face

Allow Camera access in device Settings, look toward the front camera in even light and keep your whole face visible. If the app says tracking is unsupported, that device cannot run live try-on.

The effect looks misaligned

Start facing the front camera naturally and move slowly. Switch to another look or return to the gallery to start a new session. Tell support which design and facial area look wrong.

Why does the gallery look different?

Unpainted areas are transparent so your own skin stays visible. Promotional portraits are AI-created illustrations of the designs.

My photo did not save

Allow permission to add photos and check available device storage. Wait for “Photo saved,” then check Photos. The shutter sound confirms capture was requested; it does not confirm that saving succeeded.

I cannot hear the shutter

The app requests the built-in iOS camera shutter effect. Sound behavior depends on iOS, Silent mode and device sound settings.

Which devices can I use?

iOS or iPadOS 17 or later is required. Live try-on needs ARKit face-tracking support. The app checks compatibility. iPhone uses portrait layout; iPad supports portrait and landscape.

Are all eight looks included?

Yes. The planned US price is $1.99 as a one-time app purchase, including all eight looks, with no subscription or in-app purchases. Local App Store prices may differ.

Where is the download link?

The app is in release preparation. We will add the App Store link after public availability is verified.
