Privacy policy

隐私政策 · Zodiac Face

Effective date: September 9, 2026. Developer: William Lock. This policy covers the current version of the app and this product website.

Camera and face data

With your Camera permission, the app uses Apple ARKit to process camera frames and estimated face geometry, eye positions and movement on your device. This information is used only to align and animate the selected selected face effect. The app does not use face data to identify you, create an identity profile, advertise, or track you across apps.

The developer does not receive camera frames or face data. The app does not upload, sell, share or persist face meshes or face-tracking measurements. Temporary frames and geometry are used in app memory for rendering; they are not saved as a face-data archive.

Photos you choose to save

When you tap capture, the app creates a still image of the camera scene with the effect and asks for permission to add it to your Photos library. It does not request access to browse your existing library. A saved image may include your face and surroundings. The app does not upload that image to the developer.

Photos remain in your library until you delete them. Your Apple Photos, backup or iCloud settings may sync images through Apple's services. You control those settings and any later sharing; this is separate from the app's on-device processing.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The current app has no account system, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or developer-operated backend. It does not request contacts, location or microphone access. It does not collect camera or face information for analytics.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives the address, message and attachments you choose to send and uses them to respond and troubleshoot. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by email. Please avoid sending personal photos or face data unless you deliberately choose to share them for help.

Website hosting

This website adds no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies or external embeds. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for hosting and security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke Camera and Photos permissions in device Settings. Without Camera permission, live try-on is unavailable; the gallery can still be browsed. Delete saved images in Photos and remove the app to remove its local app container. To contact the developer about privacy, email wlock88@icloud.com.

Children and changes

The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a backend or account. Parents and guardians should supervise camera use and photo sharing. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those practices take effect.
